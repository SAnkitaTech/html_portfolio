# The T568 Wire Parade™ 🎉

Created by Ankita Srivastava

Original T568A/T568B study story and mnemonic, October 12, 2026.

## Before the parade

Before the parade even began, everyone knew two things:

🍏 T568A (T-Apple) always liked Green.

🍊 T568B (TB) always liked Orange.

There were four original colors: Green, Orange, Blue, and Brown.

Orange and Green were friendly rivals who were always competing to be chosen first.

🍊 Orange vs. 🍏 Green

Blue was the fair angel 👼 of the parade, always making sure everyone followed the rules and guarding the middle spots.

Brown, the color of the ground, was grounded enough not to mind coming last. 🤎🌍

Each color always carried its own white cushion wherever it went:

- Green carried a White-Green cushion.
- Orange carried a White-Orange cushion.
- Blue carried a White-Blue cushion.
- Brown carried a White-Brown cushion.

Green secretly wished TB would choose him, while Orange hoped T568A would choose her.

But things didn't go their way.

T568A preferred Green, while T568B preferred Orange.

Ever since then, whichever color wasn't chosen first kept trying to sneak into the parade and claim a better spot.

## T568A: T-Apple Chooses Green 🍏

When it was TA's turn to organize the parade, Green and Orange both wanted to lead.

But T-Apple chose Green.

Green proudly laid down his white cushion and then took his place.

White-Green, Green

Orange wasn't happy about being left behind, so she tried to sneak into the parade.

Thinking nobody would notice, she quietly placed her cushion on the parade route.

White-Orange

For a moment, it looked like Orange might get away with it.

But suddenly Blue the fair angel saw what was happening.

“Stop! That's not fair! You weren't chosen to lead.”

Blue rushed into the middle to stop the cheating so quickly that he forgot his own cushion.

He jumped straight into position:

Blue

A moment later, his faithful White-Blue cushion came running after him:

White-Blue

With the rules restored, Blue nodded and said:

“Now that's fair. Orange, you may continue.”

So Orange stepped into the place she had already saved.

Orange

Meanwhile, Brown watched quietly from the back.

Being the color of the ground, Brown was grounded enough not to mind coming last.

So Brown laid down his white cushion and took the final position.

White-Brown, Brown

### T568A Order

White-Green, Green, White-Orange, Blue, White-Blue, Orange, White-Brown, Brown

## T568B: TB Chooses Orange 🍊

This time, TB was organizing the parade.

TB chose Orange to lead.

Orange proudly laid down her white cushion and took the first two spots.

White-Orange, Orange

Now Green felt disappointed.

He thought he had found a shortcut. Quietly, he placed his cushion on the parade route before anyone noticed.

White-Green

For a moment, it looked like Green might sneak ahead.

But Blue the fair angel caught him immediately.

“Not so fast! The rules are the rules.”

Blue charged into the middle to stop the confusion.

In his rush to restore order, he forgot his own cushion and took his place first:

Blue

Seconds later, his faithful White-Blue cushion caught up with him:

White-Blue

With the parade back in order, Green was finally allowed to continue.

Green

And just like every other parade, Brown quietly put down his white cushion and took the last spot.

After all, Brown was the color of the ground, making him grounded enough to come last.

White-Brown, Brown

### T568B Order

White-Orange, Orange, White-Green, Blue, White-Blue, Green, White-Brown, Brown

## Super-Short Memory Trick

- A = Apple = Green goes first. 🍏
- B = Orange goes first. 🍊
- Every color brings its white cushion. 🤍
- Blue is the fair angel who guards the middle spots and rushes in to restore order. 👼
- Blue forgets his cushion during the rush, so Blue comes before White-Blue. 👼
- Brown is the color of the ground, so he's grounded enough to come last. 🌍
- Orange and Green are rivals competing to be chosen first. 🍊🍏
